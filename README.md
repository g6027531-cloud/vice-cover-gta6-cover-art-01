VICE COVER turns your photo and a caption into GTA-poster-style cover art — a
multi-panel poster with bold outlined lettering and a Vice City sunset or neon
colour grade — and exports it as a PNG to share. It runs entirely offline in
a desktop browser: your photos never leave your machine.

## How it works

1. **Photo** — choose a photo from your machine, drop it onto the preview,
   or try one of the three built-in sample photos. Click any panel in the
   preview to pick it, then crop it with the zoom and position sliders, fill
   every panel with the same photo, or clear it.
2. **Layout** — Hero + 2, Grid 2 × 2, or Hero + strip.
3. **Colour grade** — Sunset, Neon, Noir, or Miami.
4. **Words** — edit the title and subtitle; the title is drawn in bold
   outlined gradient lettering with a retro sun.
5. **Export PNG** — 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350, or 1920 × 1080, in one click.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

No build step, no network requests: `app/index.html` and everything it loads
lives under `app/`.

## Originality

Everything here is original: no Rockstar Games logo, artwork, or fonts. The
lettering uses system font stacks and canvas-drawn outlines; the sample
photos were made with an image model for this entry. Licensed MIT.
